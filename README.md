# Rasa Tempo Dulu 🍘🇮🇩

**Rasa Tempo Dulu** is a traditional food showcase website created to introduce and preserve the stories, flavors, and cultural heritage of Indonesian traditional snacks.

The website presents a collection of traditional foods through dedicated pages, allowing visitors to discover different local culinary products and learn more about their unique characteristics.

## ✨ Features

* 🍘 **Traditional Food Showcase**

  * Alen-Alen
  * Geti Wijen
  * Keripik Tempe
  * Madu Mongso
  * Manco
  * Sale Pisang

* 📖 **Food Information**

  * Traditional food descriptions
  * Product information
  * Local culinary stories
  * Visual presentation of each food

* 🖼️ **Image Gallery**

  * Food photography
  * Product visuals
  * Attractive image-based presentation

* 📱 **Responsive Design**

  * Desktop-friendly interface

  * Responsive navigation

* 📩 **Contact Page**

  * Contact information

* ℹ️ **About Page**

  * Project introduction
  * Background and purpose of the website

## 🍽️ Featured Traditional Foods

### Alen-Alen

A traditional snack presented as part of the local culinary collection featured on the website.

### Geti Wijen

A traditional sesame-based snack showcasing the variety of Indonesian local flavors.

### Keripik Tempe

A crispy traditional snack made from tempe, representing one of Indonesia's well-known local food products.

### Madu Mongso

A traditional sweet snack included in the collection of local Indonesian culinary products.

### Manco

A traditional local snack presented as part of the cultural and culinary heritage showcased by this project.

### Sale Pisang

A traditional banana-based snack that represents the diversity of Indonesian traditional food.

## 🎯 Project Purpose

The main purpose of **Rasa Tempo Dulu** is to introduce traditional Indonesian foods through a simple and accessible digital platform.

The project aims to:

* Preserve knowledge about traditional foods
* Introduce local culinary products to younger generations
* Showcase Indonesian culinary diversity
* Provide an attractive digital presentation of traditional snacks
* Promote appreciation for local culinary heritage
